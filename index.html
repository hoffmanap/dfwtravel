03 — Reading the numbers

Notes on interpretation

Three things worth knowing before treating any single number here as ground truth.

Built from El Paso-flagged devices in the visitor-home panel, joined to pathing data restricted to a 24-hour window following arrival at a DFW-metro airport polygon. Destination municipality assigned by point-in-polygon match against DFW Metroplex city boundaries. Repeat pings from the same device in the same city on the same local date are counted once. McKinney National Airport recorded no matched arrivals in this dataset and is omitted from the airport filter.
